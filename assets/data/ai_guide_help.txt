CareerPath AI Guide — app help

Q: What is the AI Guide?
A: The AI Guide answers questions about streams, career paths, recommended books, institutes and job sectors using the CareerPath Explore data in the app. It does not guess missing details such as fees, cut-offs or salaries.

Q: How do I ask by voice?
A: In the AI Guide tab, tap the Talk button next to the message box to start a voice conversation. Speak naturally; the guide answers aloud and keeps listening for your next question. Tap the X on the voice bar to end the conversation. The microphone icon inside the message box types a single spoken question instead.

Q: Can I interrupt the guide while it is speaking?
A: Yes. Start speaking and it stops to listen. You can turn this off in Voice settings from the menu at the top of the AI Guide.

Q: How do I change the voice?
A: Open the menu at the top of the AI Guide, choose Voice settings and pick a voice. Tap the play button to hear a preview first.

Q: How do I turn off spoken answers?
A: In Voice settings, switch off Spoken answers. Replies still appear as text.

Q: Which languages can I speak?
A: English and Hindi, plus several Indian languages in voice conversations. The guide replies in the language you speak.

Q: Where can I browse careers myself?
A: Use the Explore tab to browse streams and career paths step by step. Tap a source chip under an answer to open that career path in Explore.

Q: How do I save a career path?
A: Open a career path in Explore and use the bookmark option. Saved items appear in the Saved tab.

Q: How do I start a new chat?
A: Tap the new chat icon at the top of the AI Guide and confirm. The current conversation is cleared.

Q: Is my conversation private?
A: Conversations stay on your device. To generate answers, your question and the matching CareerPath records are sent to Google's Gemini service. Do not share personal details such as phone numbers or addresses.
