## Long Description
### About CareerPath
CareerPath is a structured career guidance mobile application designed to help students—particularly those in rural and semi-urban areas—explore academic and professional paths in an organized, hierarchical format.

Unlike search engines or AI assistants that only answer what you know to ask, CareerPath presents a complete map of possibilities, ensuring you discover the full landscape of relevant career options.

**With CareerPath you can:**
• **Explore Structured Paths:** Navigate through a hierarchical tree of careers across Science, Commerce, and Arts.
• **Drill Down to Details:** Move from broad streams to specific specializations with all the supporting info you need.
• **Curated Resources:** Access recommended literature, relevant educational institutes (with city and website), and job sector descriptions for every path.
• **Focus on Affordability:** Identify trust-run institutes and scholarship-supported pathways to find realistic options for your background.
• **Personalized Experience:** Set your stream and preferences to receive tailored suggestions immediately upon opening the app.
• **Organize Your Journey:** Track your browsing history and bookmark important career nodes for quick access later.

Whether you're deciding on a stream after school or looking for your next professional specialization, CareerPath provides the clarity and structure needed to make one of the most consequential decisions of your life.

### Who is this app for?
CareerPath is designed for students and young professionals who want to:
• Navigate the complex world of academic and professional choices with confidence.
• Discover high-potential career paths they might not have been aware of.
• Find affordable educational pathways and financial-support opportunities.
• Access a unified, domain-specific guidance platform that replaces fragmented web searches.

**Note:** This is a student-developed guidance platform designed to provide structure and information for career planning. It does not provide direct courses or placement services—it is a tool for discovery and organization to help you navigate and own your career journey.

## App Review Information
**Notes for Reviewer:**  
This app is designed for students to explore career paths in a structured way.
- The app stores user preferences (name, stream selection, affordability toggle) **locally on the device** using SharedPreferences and SQLite.
- **No login or authentication** is required to access the core career guidance content.
- The app communicates with a backend API to fetch career data, but **no personally identifiable information (PII)** is transmitted to the server.
- Firebase Analytics is integrated for anonymous usage tracking to improve content priorities.
